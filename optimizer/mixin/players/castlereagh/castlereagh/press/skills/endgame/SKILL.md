# Endgame

The game ends after the autumn movement of the end year shown in the briefing. Only the
centres you own then score. Read the briefing's scoring line for the exact rule.

- Every centre you add near the end is worth more than any promise you keep. In the final
  autumn, the reputation value of keeping a promise is gone.
- Do not plan or negotiate offensives for years after the end year.
- Under the default sum-of-squares score, taking a centre from the **leader** raises your
  share twice: you grow and their square shrinks. Prefer leader centres with `center_values`.
- Do not leave home centres empty in the final autumn; set `risk` to 0.5 or higher if a
  neighbour can reach them.
