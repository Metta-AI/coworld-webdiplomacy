# 1901 openings

Spring 1901: everyone talks. Aim for two non-aggression agreements and one clear target.

- **England**: Agree with France on the Channel (ENG as DMZ). Take NWY; keep Germany friendly.
- **France**: DMZ the Channel with England; Burgundy as DMZ with Germany. Take SPA, POR, then BEL.
- **Germany**: Burgundy DMZ with France, a quiet border with Russia (no SIL/PRU moves). Take DEN, HOL.
- **Italy**: Tyrolia/Venice DMZ with Austria; consider the Lepanto with Austria against Turkey.
- **Austria**: Galicia DMZ with Russia; Italy friendly. Take SER, GRE.
- **Turkey**: Black Sea as DMZ with Russia; Bulgaria first.
- **Russia**: Galicia DMZ with Austria; Black Sea DMZ with Turkey; Sweden in the north.

Put each agreed DMZ in `forbid_moves_into` and in a note.
